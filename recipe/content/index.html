Recipe

Brown Butter Chocolate Chip Cookies

Servings

The final recipe page will have adjustable serving sizes. It will start at 1x and allow the user to increase the recipe from there.

1x   2x   3x

Recipe Timeline

  • Brown and cool butter: about 20 minutes
  • Prepare cookie dough: about 15 minutes
  • Chill cookie dough: 1 to 2 hours
  • Preheat oven and prepare cookies: about 10 minutes
  • Bake cookies: 8 to 10 minutes
  • Cool on baking sheet: 2 minutes

Ingredients

  • 10 tablespoons (140g) salted butter
  • 1 1/2 cups (215g) all-purpose flour
  • 3/4 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 2/3 cup (145g) packed light brown sugar
  • 1/3 cup (65g) granulated sugar
  • 1 large egg, at room temperature
  • 1 tablespoon (15ml) milk
  • 1 teaspoon (5ml) pure vanilla extract
  • 3 oz (85g) dark chocolate, chopped into chunks
  • 1/2 cup (85g) semisweet chocolate chips

Dietary Substitutions

  • Gluten-free: Substitute the all-purpose flour with a 1-to-1 gluten-free baking flour.
  • Dairy-free: Use a dairy-free butter alternative, non-dairy milk, and dairy-free chocolate.
  • Nut-free: This recipe does not contain nuts, but check the labels on the chocolate and other ingredients if there is a nut allergy.

Substitutions may slightly change the taste or texture of the cookies.

Instructions

  1. Brown the butter in a saucepan until brown specks appear and a golden foam forms on top. Pour the butter into a large mixing bowl and let it cool for about 10 minutes. Place it in the freezer for about 10 minutes until it becomes cool and thick, but not hard.
  2. Combine the flour, baking soda, and salt in a medium bowl. Whisk the ingredients together until they are evenly combined.
  3. Add the brown sugar and granulated sugar to the cooled brown butter. Mix gently until the mixture becomes thick and resembles wet sand.
  4. Add the egg, milk, and vanilla. Mix until everything is completely combined and the mixture looks creamy.
  5. Add the flour mixture and mix until it is mostly combined. Add the chocolate chunks and chocolate chips and continue mixing until they are evenly distributed throughout the dough.
  6. Cover the bowl and refrigerate the cookie dough for 1 to 2 hours.
  7. Preheat the oven to 375°F and line baking sheets with parchment paper. Roll the dough into balls and place them about 3 inches apart on the baking sheets. Do not flatten the dough.
  8. Bake for 8 to 10 minutes, until the cookies are golden on top, browned around the edges, and still soft in the middle. Let them cool on the baking sheet for 2 minutes before moving them to a wire rack.

Changes for Final Recipe Page

Based on my peer evaluations and research, I want to make the final recipe page easier to use while someone is baking. I want to include adjustable serving sizes that start at 1x and can increase from there. The ingredient amounts will change depending on the serving size selected.

I also want to include a visual timeline that shows how long each stage of the recipe will take. This will help the user know how much time they need before starting the recipe, especially because the cookie dough needs to chill for 1 to 2 hours.

The ingredient section will also include possible substitutions for common dietary restrictions. I want these options to be easy to find without distracting from the original recipe.
