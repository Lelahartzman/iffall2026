01 / INTRODUCTION

More than the
Lady with the Lamp.

Florence Nightingale was a nurse, reformer, and pioneer in the use of statistics. Her work during the Crimean War helped transform hospital care and established the foundations of modern nursing.

Through her dedication to improving sanitation, hospital conditions, and nursing education, Nightingale transformed the way patients were cared for. Her influence continues to shape healthcare around the world.
